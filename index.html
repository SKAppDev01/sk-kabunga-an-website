About the App

Built to make SK management simpler, organized, accessible, and reliable.

The SK Kabunga-an Official App is designed to assist the Sangguniang Kabataan of Barangay Kabunga-an in managing important information and day-to-day operations from an Android device.

The application follows an offline-first approach, allowing important features and records to remain available even without a continuous internet connection.

App Features

Tools created to support the day-to-day operations of SK Kabunga-an.

Youth Registry

Register, update, search, and manage youth information and records.

Finance

Manage budgets, allocations, expenses, and financial records.

Activities

Create activities, manage participants, and record attendance.

Projects

Organize SK projects, participants, expenses, and progress.

Meetings

Create and manage SK meeting information and schedules.

QR Tools

Use QR codes for faster attendance and easier youth registration.

Getting Started

Create an Account

Create your local SK account before signing in to the application.

Create Account screen of the SK Kabunga-an Official App.

1

Enter a Username

Type the username you want to use for your local SK account. The username must contain at least 3 characters.

2

Create a Password

Enter a password with at least 6 characters. Choose a password that you can remember but that other people cannot easily guess.

3

Confirm Your Password

Enter the same password again in the Confirm Password field. Both password entries must match.

4

Choose a Recovery Question

Select a recovery question that you can answer later if you forget your password.

5

Enter Your Recovery Answer

Type the answer to your selected recovery question. The recovery answer must contain at least 3 characters.

6

Tap Create Account

Review your information, then tap Create Account. After the account is created successfully, continue to the login screen and sign in.

Account Recovery Remember your recovery question and answer. You may need them if you forget your password. Keep your account credentials private.
Getting Started

Sign In to Your Account

Use the login screen to access your local SK account on the device.

Login screen of the SK Kabunga-an Official App.

1

Enter a Username

Tap the Username field and enter the username you created for your local SK account.

2

Enter Your Password

Tap the Password field and enter your account password. Use the eye icon if you want to show or hide the password while typing.

3

Tap Sign In

After entering the correct username and password, tap the blue Sign In button to access the application.

4

Forgot Your Password?

If you cannot remember your password, tap Forgot password? and follow the account recovery process.

5

Create an Account if Needed

If you do not have an account yet, tap Create account at the bottom of the login screen to begin account registration.

Offline Account The login screen is for your local SK account. Keep your username and password private and protect access to the device where your records are stored.

Complete Profile screen of the SK Kabunga-an Official App.

Privacy & Offline Use

Designed with local access and responsible handling of SK information in mind.

The SK Kabunga-an Official App follows an offline-first approach. Important application records are designed to remain available on the device even when a continuous internet connection is unavailable.

Users should handle youth information, financial records, documents, account credentials, backups, and other SK data responsibly. Access to the device and application should be limited to authorized users.
