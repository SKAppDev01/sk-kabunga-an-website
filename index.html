An offline-first management application designed to help Sangguniang Kabataan officials manage youth records, activities, projects, finances, meetings, documents, attendance, and other SK operations.

About the App

Built to make SK management simpler, organized, accessible, and reliable.

The SK Kabunga-an App is designed to assist the Sangguniang Kabataan of Barangay Kabunga-an in managing important information and daily operations from an Android device.


The application follows an offline-first approach, allowing important features and records to remain available even without a continuous internet connection.

App Features

Some of the main tools available in the SK Kabunga-an App.

Youth Registry

Register and manage youth information and records.

Finance

Manage budgets, allocations, expenses, and financial records.

Activities

Create activities, manage participants, and record attendance.

Projects

Organize SK projects, participants, expenses, and progress.
