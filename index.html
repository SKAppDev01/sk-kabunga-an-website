About the App

Built to make SK management simpler, organized, accessible, and reliable.

The SK Kabunga-an Official App is designed to assist the Sangguniang Kabataan of Barangay Kabunga-an in managing important information and day-to-day operations from an Android device.

The application follows an offline-first approach, allowing important features and records to remain available even without a continuous internet connection.

App Features

Tools created to support the day-to-day operations of SK Kabunga-an.

Youth Registry

Register, update, search, and manage youth information and records.

Finance

Manage budgets, allocations, expenses, and financial records.

Activities

Create activities, manage participants, and record attendance.

Projects

Organize SK projects, participants, expenses, and progress.

Meetings

Create and manage SK meeting information and schedules.

QR Tools

Use QR codes for faster attendance and easier youth registration.

Documents

Organize SK documents and keep important records easier to access.

Inventory

Record and manage SK items, equipment, and returns.

Data Management

Use backup, import, and transfer tools to help protect local records.

How to Use the App

Getting Started

Create an Account

Follow these steps when opening the app for the first time or when creating a new local account on the device.

Account Creation Screenshot Put your screenshot in the same folder as this HTML file and name it: account-creation.png

Account Creation screen of the SK Kabunga-an Official App.

1

Enter a Username

Type the username you want to use for your local SK account. The username must contain at least 3 characters.

2

Create a Password

Enter a password with at least 6 characters. Choose something that you can remember but that other people cannot easily guess.

3

Confirm the Password

Enter the same password again in the Confirm Password field. Both password entries must match.

4

Select a Recovery Question

Choose a recovery question that you can answer later if you forget your password.

5

Enter the Recovery Answer

Type your answer to the selected recovery question. The answer should contain at least 3 characters.

6

Tap Create Account

Review the information you entered, then tap the Create Account button. When the account is created successfully, continue to the login screen.

Important Keep your username, password, recovery question, and recovery answer private. Your recovery information may be needed if you forget your password.
Account Created Successfully After successful account creation, tap Continue and sign in using the username and password you created.

Privacy & Offline Use

Designed with local access and responsible handling of SK information in mind.

The SK Kabunga-an Official App follows an offline-first design. Users should still handle youth information, financial records, documents, account credentials, backups, and other SK data responsibly. Access to the device and application should be limited to authorized users.
